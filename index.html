The tie-in solver

Measure two pipe ends. Get the whole spool.

Step 1

Shoot it

Pull a string line or tape between the two open ends: how far over, how far up, how far ahead.

Step 2

Punch it in

Pick the pipe size and elbows you have on the truck. Long radius, short radius or 3R.

Step 3

Mark and cut

Pipewright tells you which ell to cut down, to what angle, where to set the throat and the heel mark, and the spool length.

What's in the box

Every tool works offline, so it's there in the ditch, the pipe rack or the tank farm.

/ Offsets

Simple and rolling offsets, equal spread, set and run, travel and cut lengths.

/ Fittings

Takeoffs and cut-down marks for 1.5R and 3R ells at any angle, miters, compound miters, saddles and laterals with wrap templates.

/ Pipeline

Sag and side bends, grade, joint tally with heat numbers and voice entry, cut list with drops.

/ Hydrotest

Line volume, fill time, test pressure, high and low points, % SMYS check, and a hold log that prints a signed PDF.

/ Flanges

Torque by stud, lube and gasket, bolting by class, two-holing a rolled flange, star patterns.

/ Weld settings

Amps by rod size, TIG tungsten and gas, MIG and flux core volts and wire speed.

/ Smart level

Zero it on the pipe, read degrees, grade and inch per foot, and save readings with one tap.

/ Photo stamp

Job photos stamped with job name, date, GPS location and your company logo.

/ Charts

Pipe schedules, fitting dimensions, feet-inch-fraction calculator, decimal charts.
