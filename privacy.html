Pipewright Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Pipewright pipefitting calculator for iPhone and Android

Short version: Pipewright does not collect, sell or share your information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Who we are

Pipewright is made by Ryan Robinette. Questions about this policy: pipewrightapp@gmail.com.

What Pipewright stores, and where

The measurements, saved spools, joint tallies, projects, settings and logos you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Phone features the app can use

  • Camera and photos: used only when you take or choose a photo for the GPS photo stamp. Stamped photos are saved to your phone's photo library. We never receive them.
  • Location: used only to print GPS coordinates on a photo you are stamping, and only after you allow it. Your location is not stored by us or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone and speech: used only when you tap Talk to enter numbers by voice. The words are turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (from Apple or Google), which is covered by their privacy policies. Pipewright does not record or keep the audio.
  • Motion sensors: used by the Level tool to read angles on your screen. Nothing is stored or sent.

You can turn any of these permissions off at any time in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Things you choose to send

When you share a spool, export a file or send a problem report, the app opens your phone's own share sheet or email. What gets sent, and to whom, is up to you. A problem report includes the numbers on the screen you were using and basic device details, so we can fix the issue.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

Pipewright is a tool for adult trade workers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
