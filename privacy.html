Pipewright Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Pipewright pipefitting calculator for iPhone and Android

Short version: Pipewright doesn't sell or share your information, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Your measurements, tallies and photos stay on your phone. The only things that leave it are a purchase record when you buy a plan, and a photo's GPS coordinates when the stamp looks up a street address. Both are explained below.

Who we are

Pipewright is made by Ryan Robinette. Questions about this policy: support@pipewrighttools.com.

What Pipewright stores, and where

The measurements, saved spools, joint tallies, projects, settings and logos you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Phone features the app can use

  • Camera and photos: used only when you take or choose a photo for the GPS photo stamp. Stamped photos are saved to your phone's photo library. We never receive them.
  • Location: used only for the GPS photo stamp, and only after you allow it. If the stamp shows a street address, the coordinates are used to look it up (see "Street address on photos" below). We never store or receive your location.
  • Microphone and speech: used only when you tap Talk to enter numbers by voice. The words are turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (from Apple or Google), which is covered by their privacy policies. Pipewright does not record or keep the audio.
  • Motion sensors: used by the Level tool to read angles on your screen. Nothing is stored or sent.

You can turn any of these permissions off at any time in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Things you choose to send

When you share a spool, export a file or send a problem report, the app opens your phone's own share sheet or email. What gets sent, and to whom, is up to you. A problem report includes the numbers on the screen you were using and basic device details, so we can fix the issue.

Street address on photos

When the GPS stamp shows a street address, the app looks up the address for that photo's GPS coordinates. It asks your phone's built-in map service first (from Apple or Google). If that doesn't answer, it sends the coordinates to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service. Nothing else is sent with them, and we never receive them. Nominatim is run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation under its own privacy policy. To skip the lookup, turn off Street address in the stamp settings.

Purchases

Pipewright Field Book and Pipewright Elite are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details. We use RevenueCat to confirm what you've bought and unlock it on your device. RevenueCat receives a random ID created by the app, your purchase records and basic device details. It does not receive your name or email from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

Pipewright is a tool for adult trade workers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
